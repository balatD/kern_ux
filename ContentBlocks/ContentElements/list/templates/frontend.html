The tag is chosen in tag form, not inline. An inline f:if whose condition is a *comparison* with an unbraced operand - `f:if(condition: data.x == 'number', ...)` - does not parse as a ViewHelper at all: Fluid emits it as literal text, atom.list receives that string as its tag, falls through to its default and renders a
    carrying kern-list--number. The result looks numbered and is announced as an unordered list. An inline condition that is a plain accessor is fine, which is why the same shape elsewhere works.
