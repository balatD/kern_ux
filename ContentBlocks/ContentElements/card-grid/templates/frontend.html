Card headings sit one level below the block heading, so the outline stays intact whether or not the block itself has a heading. The footer fragment is unconditional and guards its own body from the inside. A fragment wrapped in f:if is not collected as a slot at all, and a newline inside one makes the slot whitespace-truthy - which would give every dateless card an empty footer. Card only emits kern-card__footer when the slot renders something, so a card without a date stays byte for byte what it was. {card.date -> f:format.date(format: dateFormat)}
