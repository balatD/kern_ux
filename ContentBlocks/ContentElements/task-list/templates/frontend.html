Each status gets its own id and the step link points at it with aria-describedby, so a screen reader announces "Persönliche Daten, Erledigt" instead of leaving the badge as a stray label somewhere after the link. A step without a link renders as plain text rather than a dead link - that is how a step which cannot be started yet should behave. The numbering checkbox reaches the list as well as the items: without numbers the steps are a set rather than a sequence, and KERN renders that variant as a ul. Left unwired it produced an ol whose items showed no numbers at all.
