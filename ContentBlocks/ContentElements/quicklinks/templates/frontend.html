The Schnellzugriff every municipal portal opens with - "Wie können wir helfen?" on stadt-koeln.de, the four-tile row on berlin.de. It emits no markup of its own: a tile is molecule.card with an icon and a stretched link, laid out by organism.cardGrid. KERN ships no tile classes, so building a component for it would mean inventing public design surface for a pattern that composes out of what is already there. Tile headings sit one level below the block heading, so the outline stays intact whether or not the block itself has a heading.
