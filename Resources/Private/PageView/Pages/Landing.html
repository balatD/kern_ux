Themenseite: stage, then content with a side column, then a teaser row. Each column is resolved once into a variable and then printed. The guard form used by Startpage.html and Subject.html - f:if on an f:cObject inline call, followed by the same f:cObject again - renders the column twice per request, which for a non-cacheable plugin in the aside means it executes twice. The idiom here is the one Layouts/Default.html uses: resolve, test, f:format.raw. Top padding only on the stage, bottom only on the teaser row: the gap towards the middle band comes from that band's own padding, and two of them would double it.
