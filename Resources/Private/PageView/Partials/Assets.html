Asset inclusion lives in Fluid rather than TypoScript so the boolean site settings stay booleans. TypoScript constant substitution would flatten them into strings and force conditions around every include. rte.css is scoped to .kernt3-rte for the frontend and .ck-content for the editing view, so both show the same thing. It was previously wired into the CKEditor preset only, which left every rich-text block unstyled on the rendered page.
