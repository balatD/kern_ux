Standalone document: the gallery deliberately does not go through the page templates, so a component can be inspected without any page tree or editorial content existing. Heading order is h1 (page) > h2 (component) > h3 (example), which is why the heading examples are pinned to level 4 in Examples.yaml - otherwise the gallery would itself violate the rule it exists to demonstrate.
