Three fields in one fieldset, the way KERN specifies a date. Each part is a plain text input with inputmode="numeric", not type="number": a spinner on a day-of-month is meaningless, and number inputs silently drop leading zeros and accept exponents. Widths come from KERN's own modifiers - two characters for day and month, four for the year - so the fields look like what they expect rather than like full-width text boxes. The parts carry aria-invalid but not aria-describedby: the hint and the error are announced once, from the fieldset, rather than three times over. Nor do they carry the element's fluidAdditionalAttributes, which every other partial passes on. One date is three inputs here, and those attributes are singular by nature: one autocomplete token, one placeholder, one pattern. Spreading a single autocomplete="bday" across day, month and year would be worse than emitting none, because autofill would target three fields with one token. Doing this properly means mapping the parts onto bday-day, bday-month and bday-year; until then the parts stay as they were.
