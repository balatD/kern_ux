Three fields in one fieldset, the way KERN specifies a date. Each part is a plain text input with inputmode="numeric", not type="number": a spinner on a day-of-month is meaningless, and number inputs silently drop leading zeros and accept exponents. Widths come from KERN's own modifiers - two characters for day and month, four for the year - so the fields look like what they expect rather than like full-width text boxes. The parts carry aria-invalid but not aria-describedby: the hint and the error are announced once, from the fieldset, rather than three times over.
