The error summary above a felogin form. Core lists the raw property paths and error codes, which is developer output rather than something a visitor can act on. KERN puts the same information in an alert, and this one is announced: unlike an editorially placed alert it appears as the result of something the visitor just did, which is exactly the case role=alert exists for. Per-field messages are rendered by the Field partial as well. That repetition is deliberate and is what the ext:form theme does too - the summary is what a screen reader user hears first, the inline message is what they find when they reach the control.
