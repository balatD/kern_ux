One KERN field row for felogin. Partials/Form/Frontend/Field/Field.html cannot be reused here: that partial is driven by ext:form element objects, and felogin has none. What KERN's field contract is made of - the label, the error, their DOM order - comes from the same atoms the rest of the extension uses, so the only thing written a second time is the kern-form-input wrapper, not the contract. Order is label, control, error. That is what KERN prescribes and it is also the order a screen reader reads.
