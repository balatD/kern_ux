The number sits INSIDE the link. KERN moved it there in 2.7.0 because screen readers otherwise skip it, so a step announced as "Persönliche Daten" gave no clue which step it was. KERN keeps a fallback for the old shape - the number as a direct child of the item - but marks it deprecated and slated for removal, so it is not used here. kern-task-list__title wraps the step AND its status. That is not cosmetic: the wrapper is what wraps the badge onto its own line below 768px (flex-wrap: wrap, reset to nowrap from there up). Without it both sit in the item's own flex row, which never wraps - so on a phone the badge squeezed the step title instead of moving under it. kern-link--stretched turns the whole row into the click target and is what drives KERN's hover and active tint on the item; without it only the words were clickable and the row had no hover state at all.
