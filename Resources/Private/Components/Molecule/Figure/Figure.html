A fixed srcset ladder rather than a configurable one: a design system that lets every caller pick its own widths ends up generating dozens of near-identical derivatives. 480/768/1024/1440 covers the breakpoints KERN defines. alt is always emitted, even empty: an img without alt is announced by its file name, while alt="" correctly marks a decorative image (WCAG 1.1.1). Nothing to render without a source. Guarded here rather than at every call site: a collection with an empty row would otherwise take down the whole page.
