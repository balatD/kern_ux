A fixed srcset ladder rather than a configurable one: a design system that lets every caller pick its own widths ends up generating dozens of near-identical derivatives. 480/768/1024/1440 covers the breakpoints KERN defines. alt is always emitted, even empty: an img without alt is announced by its file name, while alt="" correctly marks a decorative image (WCAG 1.1.1). The long description is real, visible content, associated through aria-describedby - not the longdesc attribute the field is named after. longdesc was removed from HTML5 and is surfaced by almost nothing, so a description put there reaches nobody. Visible also means it reaches people who are not using a screen reader at all, which for a chart is most of the people who need it. It sits inside the figcaption because a figcaption has to be the figure's first or last child: a third sibling after it would be invalid, and moving the caption to the top to make room would change every existing image on every existing page. Nothing to render without a source. Guarded here rather than at every call site: a collection with an empty row would otherwise take down the whole page.
