KERN names the alert icons after the variants, so the variant is the icon name. The body is captured first so it can be left out entirely when it is empty: KERN's alert body carries a white background and the rounded bottom corners, so an empty one renders as a blank white strip under the heading. Without it the alert is the coloured row on its own, which is the form KERN's own examples use for a one-line note.
