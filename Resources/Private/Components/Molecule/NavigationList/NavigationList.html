One list, any depth. The component renders itself for a sublist rather than the header repeating a loop per level - which is what limited the navigation to two levels before, so a visitor on a third-level page could not see its siblings and the page they were on was not in the menu at all. Every item carries its children, not just the active branch: the flyout has to be able to open on hover or focus anywhere in the bar, and a panel can only open for an item whose children are in the markup.
