KERN markup for the felogin login form. The status message is an alert rather than core's heading plus paragraph, and its variant follows the key felogin sets: `error` after a failed attempt, `logout` after signing out, `welcome` otherwise. Only the error case is announced - the other two describe a state the visitor already knows they are in. Every label and message key is hoisted into an f:variable before it reaches an arguments= array. Braces inside such an array interpolate into a string, and Fluid then throws at *render* time, long after the template has parsed cleanly; see TemplateSyntaxTest::translateArgumentsAreArraysNotStrings. The section/form split is core's and is kept as is: f:form has to wrap the section, and pulling the fields out of it would render them outside the request token.
